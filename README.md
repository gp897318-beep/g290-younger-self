A make.fun entry: the single line I would send my younger self, written to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Keep the letters. One day his handwriting will be the only voice of his you have left.

The line is 86 characters — inside the competition gate of 1–200 characters, a single line,
no trailing newline. It was picked as the winner of 10 compared variants
(see `younger-self-variants.md` in the work folder): one concrete object (the letters),
one turn (handwriting becomes a voice), and a sting that stays after a single reading.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` outside the grader) and verifies it round-trip against the gate.
